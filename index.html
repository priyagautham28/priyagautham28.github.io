Hi, I'm Priyadarshini. My work is about helping robots see and understand the world the way humans do — connecting language, vision, and action so machines can reason about what they're looking at, not just detect it. I spent seven years as a full-stack/backend engineer at Boeing, Ford, and Toyota Financial Services, and I'm currently in a graduate program in AI & Machine Learning for Engineering at the University of Washington (2025–2027), applying that systems background to vision-language-action models, knowledge distillation, and robotic grasping.
