Code + results
Knowledge Distillation for Efficient Mathematical Reasoning in Compact Language Models
Distilling a Qwen3-14B reasoning teacher into compact 1–2B student models (Llama-3.2-1B, Qwen3-1.7B, Gemma-3-1B) for privacy-preserving local deployment, evaluated on GSM8K. I owned the Qwen3-14B-AWQ teacher pipeline and the Llama-3.2-1B student (QLoRA). We distilled the same GSM8K traces into three 1–2B students, each chosen to test a different hypothesis. Llama had no prior math specialization, the control for how much generic distillation alone can teach (44.3% → 50.8%). Qwen3-1.7B, same family as the teacher and with a built-in reasoning mode, was the best-case upper bound, closing to within 12.7 pp of the teacher’s 92.27%. Gemma tested whether existing math-oriented training survives a second round of generic fine-tuning the most interesting result: it regressed 51.4% → 41.0% instead of keeping its edge. Same recipe, three outcomes: Distillation is not a guaranteed upgrade: generic SFT can overwrite a model’s prior specialization instead of building on it.

MolmoAct2 × LIBERO Evaluation
Evaluated Allen AI’s MolmoAct2 VLA on LIBERO robot manipulation (3-person project. I ran the Object and Spatial suites, 1,000 episodes, plus failure/recovery analysis). Team pooled success across four suites was 97.95% (narrow 96.8–99.8% band). Failures were not uniform: they clustered on a few long, hard tasks, and the policy almost never recovered after an early mistake. High average success can hide concentrated risk.

Language-Guided Grasp Point Prediction
Comparing CLIP, Grounding DINO, and Florence-2 for predicting grasp points on objects from natural-language instructions, evaluated on the PixMo-Points dataset.I built the CLIP baseline for predicting a grasp point from a text instruction, and compared it with the team’s Grounding DINO and Florence-2 runs. CLIP was strong at linking words to the right object, but weak at pinning a pixel: about 6% success at a 50px threshold, versus 32.8% (DINO) and 22.4% (Florence-2). Vision-language models cut down brute-force geometric search, still need a localizer to turn that understanding into a stable grasp in clutter.
